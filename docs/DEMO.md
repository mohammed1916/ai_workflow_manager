# Relay demo script

Use fictional sample data or explicitly cleared material. Prefer a live Nemotron run for the final recording. Never present the offline template as AI inference.

1. **Problem (15 seconds):** “Useful work gets stuck between tabs, meeting notes, and the final handoff. Relay closes that gap while keeping the person in control.”
2. **Context (20 seconds):** Show the source shelf. Preview the fictional launch notes. If the Chrome extension has been installed and verified, capture a public page and select it.
3. **Plan (30 seconds):** Select Notes to next steps. Show Live Nemotron and the source-sharing consent. Build the workflow. Explain that Nebius-hosted NVIDIA Nemotron converts source material into concrete file actions.
4. **Review (30 seconds):** Open a proposed output. Check owners and deadlines against the notes. Edit a line and show the revision increment.
5. **Execute (20 seconds):** Approve and run. Show the activity trail and saved output hashes. Download the action list or reveal the folder in Finder.
6. **Recovery (20 seconds):** On a second plan, reject with a specific correction and show feedback carried into a new request. Alternatively modify a selected source after planning and show stale-source approval refused.
7. **Close (10 seconds):** “The model proposes. You shape the result. Relay executes the approved plan and leaves evidence.”

Show actual runs and latency. Do not make unsupported accuracy, impact, cost, or automation-coverage claims. Confirm the official submission video limit before final editing.

## Research demo: the GTS correction (draft script)

Centre the video on one documented correction. Show actual runs and latency. Do not claim a result the run did not produce.

1. **Problem (15 seconds):** a benchmark table makes one method look better. The numbers may not be comparable.
2. **Evidence (30 seconds):** open the GTS result from arXiv 2101.06861. Show its reported MAE of 2.99 at 60 minutes, the table cell it was read from, and the quote.
3. **Comparison (20 seconds):** compare with Graph WaveNet at 3.53 (arXiv 1906.00121). Show the apparent lead.
4. **Correction (40 seconds):** show Appendix D of the GTS paper, which says that version used a metric calculation that differs from the official DCRNN protocol. Show the corrected Table 4 value, 3.41. Show the correction recorded with its source, the comparisons that change, and the manuscript claims marked stale.
5. **Regeneration (20 seconds):** regenerate the affected paragraph from the corrected evidence. Show the previous text kept for comparison.
6. **Branch and export (15 seconds):** open the state before the correction as a branch. Export the ZIP.
7. **Close (10 seconds):** the model proposes, the evidence is checked, the researcher decides.

Caveats to say in the video: the correction is documented by the authors, not discovered by Relay; the evaluation reports both wins and misses; the app has been tested by its authors only.
