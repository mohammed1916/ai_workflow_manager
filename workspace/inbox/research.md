# Research — the handoff gap

Fictional discovery notes for a product demo, not a published study.

Three interviews with small product teams revealed a recurring workflow:
1. Read documents and browser tabs.
2. Copy useful parts into meeting notes.
3. Rewrite decisions into an action list.
4. Save files and tell teammates where to look.

Interview A: Losing source links made the final brief hard to trust.
Interview B: A silent overwrite erased a colleague's corrections.
Interview C: An agent reported success although the output file was empty.

Design implications: preserve citations, review exact changes, guard against stale state, and verify saved output.
